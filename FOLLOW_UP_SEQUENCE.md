# FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE — the bump and the stop

Two touches maximum. The practice's brand is that we respond, we don't chase.
A person who doesn't reply after two is not a lead, they're a stranger, and
treating them like one is what makes referrals possible later.

## Touch 1 — the original message (Day 0)

`OUTREACH.md` message, personalized. Logged in the pipeline board.

## Touch 2 — the bump (Day 4, business days only)

> Subject: same thread — reply to your original, do NOT start a new one
>
> No worries if this isn't the week for it. One line of useful anyway:
> the self-verify commands are free and need nothing from me —
> [link to ONE_PAGER appendix commands or sample report appendix]
>
> If the timing's ever right, the free 20-minute triage stands.
> This is my last note on it.

**Rules for the bump:**
- 3 sentences maximum. It exists to give, not to squeeze.
- Reply in the same thread — a new thread is spam, a reply is a courtesy
- Include something useful with no strings (the commands) — that's what makes
  the last line believable
- Send at a human hour; no scheduling tricks

## The stop (permanent, with one exception)

After the bump: **stop entirely.** Do not:
- "just circling back" a third time
- re-add them to any list
- treat a future non-response as unfinished business

The one exception: if they *engaged and went quiet mid-conversation* (booked a
call and no-showed, asked for the agreement then vanished), one final note
after 10 days, purely logistical, no pitch:

> "Closing the loop — assume timing wasn't right. The offer stands whenever.
> Nothing further from me either way."

Then closed. permanently.

## Why two is the number

- Touch 1 filters for interest; touch 2 proves the follow-through is graceful
- The "this is my last note" line only works if it's TRUE — it becomes the
  most differentiating sentence in the whole funnel
- Stopped leads stay warm years. Chased leads go cold and tell friends.
- Log every stop in the pipeline board (`OUTREACH.md`) with the date — the
  discipline is auditable, like everything else here
