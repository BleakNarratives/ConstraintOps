# EMAIL TEMPLATES — the four moments

Fill every [bracket]. Send within 24h of the triggering event; speed is part
of the product.

## 1. Intake reply (after someone responds to outreach)

> Subject: Re: [their subject] — here's how we start
>
> [Name] — good to hear from you. Before we talk, two quick things so the call
> is useful instead of exploratory:
>
> 1. Don't clean anything up. If there are chat histories, transcripts,
>    screenshots, or error messages — leave them exactly where they are.
>    They're the evidence.
> 2. Answer whatever you can of these [link to INTAKE questions or paste the
>    short form] — "don't know" is a fine answer.
>
> Then grab any slot here: [booking link] — or reply with two times that work.
> The call is 20 minutes and you'll leave knowing whether it's a real mess,
> an afternoon self-fix, or something I'm wrong for.

## 2. Report delivery

> Subject: Your [service name] report — [project/client name]
>
> [Name] — attached is your [service] report. Three things about reading it:
>
> 1. **Section 1 is the whole story** if you only read one thing.
> 2. **The appendix is the receipt.** Every finding traces to a file or
>    transcript, and the final page has the exact commands to re-run the
>    checks yourself. Please do — if anything doesn't reproduce, that's my
>    problem and I want to hear it first.
> 3. Where the report says "needs human judgment," that's the debrief call:
>    those calls are where findings become decisions.
>
> Reply with two times for the 30-minute debrief and I'll send an invite.
> Invoice attached as well: [amount], due on receipt, as agreed.

## 3. Debrief scheduling

> Subject: Debrief on your [service] report — 30 minutes
>
> [Name] — times below are [timezone]; pick one or counter both:
>
> - [Option A]
> - [Option B]
>
> Agenda, so nobody wanders: 5 min walking the top findings → 10 min on the
> "needs judgment" items (I'll have opinions, I want yours) → 10 min on the
> action list order → 5 min questions. You'll leave with the list reordered
> by what *you* decide matters first.

## 4. Testimonial ask (2–3 days after debrief, once they've acted)

> Subject: One small ask
>
> [Name] — glad the [fix/action] landed. Two asks, both small:
>
> 1. A sentence or two I can quote: what was the situation before, and what
>    did the report change? Honest beats glowing — "I was skeptical" is a
>    great opening line for a testimonial.
> 2. One person you know who's fighting with AI tools, automation, or a
>    platform maze. An introduction is worth more to me than the fee was.
>
> Either or both appreciated. And the re-verify commands are in the appendix
> whenever you want to re-run the sweep — monthly is a good cadence.
