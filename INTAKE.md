# Client Intake — ConstraintOps

Filled in during the free 20-minute triage call, or sent as a form.
Goal: locate the mess, the evidence, and the blast radius before quoting.

## 1. The situation (their words, uninterrupted)

- What happened, in your own words? (Write as much as you like.)
- When did you first suspect something was wrong?
- What made you reach out *today*?

## 2. The systems involved

- Which AI tools/agents were involved? (names: Gemini, Claude, Codex, Copilot, other)
- What do they have access to? (files, code, email, servers, payments, publishing)
- Which operating system / where do they run? (so transcripts can be located)
- Is there a git repository involved? Is it actually in use?

## 3. The evidence (they usually have more than they think)

- Do you still have the chat/session history? (Don't clean anything up first.)
- Any screenshots, exports, or emails from the tools?
- When did each system last change something, as far as you know?

## 4. The stakes

- What is the worst realistic outcome if nothing is fixed this month?
- What has this already cost (money, time, data, sleep)?
- Deadline pressure? (launch, court date, tax date, account deletion countdown)

## 5. The money moment

- Restate the mess in one sentence, back to them, calmly.
- Name the service tier that fits. Say why in one sentence.
- Quote the fixed price and turnaround. No hourly, no open-ended.

## Operator notes (never shown to client)

- Transcript paths located: ______
- Project root(s) for claim verification: ______
- First tool pass planned: `triage_report.py` / manual sweep / not applicable
- Trap check: is this actually a scam on the *client*? Refer out if yes.
- Creed check: can we reveal all of this to the client? If any finding can't be
  shared openly, re-examine why we're keeping it.
