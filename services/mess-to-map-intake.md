# Mess-to-Map — Same-Day Intake Mini-Form

The $100 entry tier ships in 24h. This form IS the intake — three questions,
paste of stuff, done. Designed so a client can complete it in under 10 minutes
and the operator can turn it around same-day.

---

## The form (send as-is; client fills the blanks)

**1. What is all this?** One or two sentences, your words:
> [ ... ]

**2. What were you hoping to find or decide?** (what would make this worth it)
> [ ... ]

**3. The stuff** — paste, attach, or link any of:
- [ ] notes / text files / chat exports
- [ ] screenshots (images fine, no need to organize them)
- [ ] folder paths (e.g. `~/Downloads/recovery-stuff/`)
- [ ] links (docs, gists, pastes)
- [ ] nothing else — that's the whole mess

One line about timing if it matters: **"I need this by ____"**

---

## Operator same-day workflow

1. **Triage the form itself (5 min):** is the mess in scope? Anything that
   smells like scam/legal/crisis → use the exits in TRIAGE_CALL_SCRIPT.md §3,
   return the fee, refer out. This check comes FIRST, before the invoice.
2. **Invoice $100** (pilot framing per the service sheet).
3. **Inventory pass** (30–60 min): catalog every item — type, date, one-line
   note. No analysis yet, just the census. Unclear items get marked "?",
   never skipped.
4. **Cluster** (30 min): group into threads; name each thread after what the
   items *do together*, not what they are ("the login problem", not "4 screenshots")
5. **Render** (30 min): 2-page report per services/mess-to-map-report.md —
   situation summary (their words), the map, what's missing, 3–5 next actions.
6. **Deliver** using EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md §2, debrief offer included.

## Hard gates (creed-enforced)

- If step 3 reveals the mess is bigger than the tier (codebase collapse,
  multi-system damage) → say so in the report, quote the right service,
  deliver the map anyway at $100. The upsell is earned by finishing honestly.
- "What's missing" section is mandatory — a map with unknown gaps is how
  people get burned twice.
- 24h clock starts at materials, not at form submission. Say which one you
  mean.
