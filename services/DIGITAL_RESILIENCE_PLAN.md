# Service Playbook — Digital Resilience Plan

**Price:** $500 flat · **Turnaround:** 1 week · **Instrument:** manual + checklist tooling (later)

## What the client buys

For non-technical people and small operators: a plain-language plan so that the next
crash, lockout, scam attempt, or runaway AI action costs hours instead of everything.

## The five pillars (assessed + fixed in this order)

1. **Backups that actually restore** — not "you have backup software" but "we restored
   a file from it, today, together." An untested backup is a rumor.
2. **Credential hygiene** — password manager, 2FA on the three accounts that can
   destroy them (email, bank, primary platform), recovery codes printed.
3. **Verification habits** — one habit drilled until automatic: *before acting on any
   AI/platform instruction, verify through a second channel.* This breaks the
   voice-command reflex: authority is claimed, not proven, until the second
   channel agrees.
4. **Scam surface reduction** — their actual exposure walked through: what they
   receive, what they click, what they authorize. Not a lecture; a checklist of the
   five changes that matter for *their* patterns.
5. **The one-page plan** — laminated-on-the-monitor simplicity: who to call, what to
   unplug, where the recovery codes are, what never to give over the phone.

## Why this tier exists in the ladder

It's the entry product for the overwhelmed: people drowning in complexity that
arrives faster than they can categorize it. It builds trust for the $750 and $1,500
tiers, and it's
almost pure checklist — high margin, low risk of overpromising.

## Tone rules

Zero jargon in the client deliverable. Every instruction: one action, one sentence,
one expected result. If a sentence needs "simply," rewrite it.
