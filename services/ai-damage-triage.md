# AI Damage Triage

## Status

Active first service.

## Who It Helps

People who were harmed or nearly harmed by:

- AI-generated destructive commands
- unsafe troubleshooting advice
- accidental resets or data loss
- confusing recovery paths
- automation that changed more than expected

## Deliverable

A short incident report that explains:

- what happened
- what likely caused the damage
- what data or system areas were affected
- what was recovered
- what risks remain
- what safety rules would prevent repeat damage

## Starting Price

Pilot range: $20-$75, barter acceptable where survival reality requires it.

## Proof Artifact

The first sample is based on the Crostini/Termina and ChromeOS Powerwash
incident history.
