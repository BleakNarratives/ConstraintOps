# Service Playbook — Platform Survival Report

**Price:** $250–$500 depending on depth · **Turnaround:** 3–5 days · **Instrument:** manual

## What the client buys

A way out of (or a way to live with) an automated-bureaucracy maze: locked account,
KYC wall, demonetization, endless chatbot support loop, appeal rejected by a form
that cannot read.

## Why this sells

Millions of people are currently accountable to systems that do not care about
them and cannot hear them. The operator's edge is knowing
the genre: these systems respond to *procedure*, not *justice*. The report
converts righteous anger into a filing strategy.

## Process

1. **Timeline reconstruction** — every event, date, ticket number, screenshot. Clients
   always have more evidence than they think; they just have it scattered.
2. **System map** — what tier handled each rejection? (human, outsourced human, LLM,
   hard-coded rule) Each tier is appealed differently.
3. **Document pack** — rewrite the client's story into the format the platform's
   escalation path actually reads: short, factual, dated, no grievance language.
4. **Escalation ladder** — ordered steps: in-platform appeal → written notice →
   regulator (with exact regulator name + filing link) → public-channel escalation
   (last resort, with risks explained).
5. **Expectation setting** — honest odds. Some mazes are unescapable; the report says
   so and pivots to exit-and-replace strategy instead of endless appeal.

## Deliverable

- One-page case summary (this alone is worth the fee — it's what they forward)
- Escalation ladder with dates and owners
- All document drafts, ready to send
- Decision tree: "if no response by day X, do Y"

## The line you never cross

You advise procedure and documents. You do not impersonate the client, file on their
behalf without explicit authorization, or promise outcomes. The maze is hostile;
your practice stays clean inside it.
