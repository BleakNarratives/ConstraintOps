# Research-to-Opportunity Brief

**Status:** DORMANT — stub filled compactly, deliberately NOT pushed. Lowest
near-term earning potential of the four: hours of research per deliverable,
unprovable ROI for the client, buyers are least able to verify the work's
value in advance. Do not invest more here until ≥3 clients ask for it or
another service surfaces demand.

**Price (if activated):** $400–600 · **Turnaround:** 1 week
**Instrument:** manual research; web_search/read_url; no paid APIs

## Offer (one sentence)

> "You give me a topic, market, or problem area; I give you a grounded brief
> on where the real opportunities are, ranked, with the constraints stated."

## Deliverable

1. Executive summary (1 page)
2. Top 3 opportunities, each with: who pays, why now, minimum viable first
   step, kill criteria
3. Constraint ledger: money/KYC/hardware/time requirements per opportunity
4. Anti-recommendations: what looks attractive but isn't, and why
5. Sources appendix (every claim linked)

## Why it's dormant

- The buyer has no way to evaluate the brief until months later — weak
  trust product for a no-name practice
- Research hours don't compress; margin is worst of the four services
- Its real near-term use is *internal*: feeding the other services' client
  conversations, not being sold standalone

## Activation triggers (any one)

- A client of another service asks "what else could I do with this?" twice
- Codebase Sprawl Triage finds a client whose real question is strategic,
  not technical
- A referral source starts sending people with ideas instead of messes
