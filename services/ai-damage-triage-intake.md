# AI Damage Triage Intake

Use this when someone asks for help after an AI assistant, script, command, or
tool caused damage or confusion.

## Short Pitch

I turn AI/tool/computer damage into a clear incident report and recovery plan.
Send what happened, what you ran, screenshots or transcripts if you have them,
and what you are trying to recover. I will give you a plain-language report:
what likely happened, what is risky, what to avoid, and the next safest actions.

## Intake Questions

1. What were you trying to do?
2. What command, button, link, script, or instruction caused the problem?
3. What changed afterward?
4. What files, accounts, devices, or folders might be affected?
5. What have you already tried?
6. What do you most need back?
7. Do you have screenshots, terminal history, logs, chat transcripts, or file
   lists?

## Evidence Checklist

- exact commands
- screenshots
- chat transcript snippets
- terminal history
- error messages
- file tree before/after, if available
- storage usage before/after
- recovery steps already attempted

## Report Sections

- Executive Summary
- Timeline
- Risky Actions
- Impact
- Recovery Status
- Most Likely Causes
- Next Safe Actions
- Prevention Rules

## Red Flags

Pause and warn clearly if the next action includes:

- factory reset
- Powerwash
- `destroy`
- `delete`
- `rm -rf`
- formatting a drive
- deleting a VM/container
- changing partitions
- broad cleanup scripts
