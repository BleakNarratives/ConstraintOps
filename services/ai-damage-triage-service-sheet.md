# ConstraintOps: AI Damage Triage

## What This Is

AI Damage Triage is a plain-language incident report for people harmed or
confused by AI-generated advice, shell commands, reset flows, automation, or bad
technical troubleshooting.

It answers four questions:

- What happened?
- What is still risky?
- What should be recovered first?
- What rules prevent this from happening again?

## Who It Helps

- People who lost files after following AI advice
- People who ran a scary command and need to know what it did
- People recovering from resets, broken Linux environments, or damaged project
  folders
- People with messy screenshots, terminal history, notes, and no clear map

## What You Send

- Screenshots
- Chat transcript snippets
- Terminal history
- Error messages
- A short description of what changed
- A list of what you most need back

Private files are not required unless they are directly relevant.

## What You Get

- 2-5 page incident report
- Timeline of likely events
- Risk classification
- Recovery priority list
- Next safest actions
- Prevention checklist

## Starting Pilot Offer

Low-cost pilot: $20-$75 or barter where necessary.

The first goal is clarity, not a perfect forensic reconstruction.

## Boundaries

This is not legal advice, certified digital forensics, or guaranteed data
recovery.

This is practical triage: organize the mess, identify the danger, reduce repeat
harm, and make the next move safer.
