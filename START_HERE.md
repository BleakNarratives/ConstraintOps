# START HERE

ConstraintOps is a manual-first service project for turning broken, messy,
low-resource technical situations into clear operating plans.

It does not require hosting, paid APIs, KYC services, local LLMs, or automation
to begin.

## Current Direction

Start with AI Damage Triage.

One-sentence offer:

I turn AI/tool/computer damage into a clear incident report and recovery plan.

## Use These Files In Order

1. Read `services/ai-damage-triage-service-sheet.md`.
2. Show `samples/ai-damage-triage/crostini-powerwash-incident-sample.md` as the
   proof sample.
3. Send or paste `outreach/ai-damage-triage-short-note.md`.
4. Use `services/ai-damage-triage-intake.md` to collect details.
5. Fill out `services/ai-damage-triage-report-template.md`.

## Rule

Do not build a platform before one outside human reacts to the offer.

## First Pilot

Look for one person with one messy technical incident.

Offer a low-cost or barter pilot:

"I am doing low-cost pilot reports. Send me what happened and I will turn it
into a clear incident report and next-step plan."

## Safety Boundary

ConstraintOps does practical triage. It is not legal advice, certified digital
forensics, guaranteed recovery, or a promise to fix every damaged system.

The value is clarity under constraint.
