# AI Damage Triage Outreach Note

Short version:

I help people untangle tech damage from bad AI advice, destructive commands,
resets, broken Linux environments, and messy recovery situations.

If you send screenshots, terminal history, chat snippets, or a short description
of what happened, I can turn it into a clear incident report: what likely
happened, what is still risky, what to avoid, and the next safest steps.

I am doing low-cost pilot reports right now while I build the service. The goal
is practical clarity, not expensive corporate forensics.
