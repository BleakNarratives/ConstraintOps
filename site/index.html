The one thing that cannot be argued with

Every AI tool keeps a written record of what it claimed to do. Every claim about files — created, modified, fixed — is a checkable statement. Checking it is cheap, fast, and changes nothing on your system.

So there are only two possible outcomes, and both are good for you:

  • The claims check out. You now know your work is real, instead of hoping. That certainty is worth more than it costs.
  • The claims don't check out. You just found the problem before it found you — before the deadline, the client demo, the launch, the loss.

There is no third outcome. "Ignore it and hope" is the only losing move on the board. One recent sweep of a month's worth of AI sessions found 200 file claims that did not exist. Checking is not paranoia. It's arithmetic.
