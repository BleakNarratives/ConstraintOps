# Next 72 Hours

## Goal

Turn ConstraintOps from an idea into a tiny usable service package.

No money required. No KYC required. No hosting required. No paid APIs required.

## Day 1: Package The Proof

1. Read the public sample report out loud once.
2. Remove anything that sounds too personal, too speculative, or too hard to
   prove.
3. Keep the blunt technical parts.
4. Make sure the sample answers:
   - what happened
   - why it mattered
   - what should happen next

Done when:

- the sample can be shown to a stranger without exposing private details

## Day 2: Prepare The Offer

1. Use `services/ai-damage-triage-service-sheet.md` as the service page.
2. Use `services/ai-damage-triage-intake.md` as the client intake script.
3. Use `outreach/ai-damage-triage-short-note.md` as the first message.
4. Decide what payment or barter is realistic for the first pilot.

Done when:

- one person can understand the offer in under 30 seconds

## Day 3: Find One Pilot

Look for one person with one messy technical incident.

Possible places:

- people already asking for help in communities you can access
- local contacts
- students
- small business owners
- people recovering files, accounts, devices, or broken projects

Offer:

"I am doing low-cost pilot reports. Send me what happened and I will turn it
into a clear incident report and next-step plan."

Done when:

- one real person has been asked
- their response is recorded
- the offer is improved based on what confused them

## Rule

Do not build more system until one outside human reacts to the offer.
