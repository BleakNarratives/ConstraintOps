# AI Damage Triage Roadmap

## Goal

Create one credible sample report that proves ConstraintOps can reconstruct an
AI-caused or AI-assisted system damage incident and convert it into a prevention
plan.

## Why This First

This is the strongest starting point because:

- the incident is real
- the harm is understandable
- the deliverable does not require paid APIs
- the sample can be made from memory and local evidence
- it matches the ConstraintOps thesis directly

## Phase 1: Sample Skeleton

Create a report with these sections:

- Executive Summary
- Incident Timeline
- Destructive or High-Risk Actions
- Impact Assessment
- Recovery Status
- Root Cause Analysis
- Prevention Rules
- Client-Safe Recommendations

## Phase 2: Evidence Pass

Add only evidence that is available without risky digging:

- remembered command names
- recovered storage amounts
- local cleanup observations
- safe general ChromeOS/Crostini command classifications

Do not overclaim exact facts that are not documented.

## Phase 3: Service Template

Turn the sample into a reusable client template:

- intake questions
- evidence checklist
- report format
- red/yellow/green risk scale
- final recommendation block

## Phase 4: First Pilot

Use the sample to offer a small paid or barter pilot:

- "I can turn your AI/tool/computer mess into a clean incident report and next
  action plan."

## Definition Of Done

- One readable sample report exists
- One reusable blank template exists
- One short pitch exists
- Nothing depends on KYC, hosting, paid APIs, or local LLMs
