# WINTER CASH PLAN — minimum income path, zero marketing budget

Assumption: goal is first revenue inside 2–3 weeks, ~$500–1,500 total by deep
winter, using only what exists: the tooling, the docs, free platforms, and a
human who can send messages. No ads, no hosting costs, no KYC beyond a payment
receiver.

## Week 1 — contact (the only week that matters)

| Day | Action | Cost | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Send OUTREACH.md message to prospects 1–3 | $0 | 3 signals in flight |
| 2 | Post ONE_PAGER (condensed) in 2 communities you already belong to | $0 | 2 more signals |
| 3 | Send to prospects 4–5; check replies once | $0 | pipeline full |
| 4–7 | Free triage calls for anyone who replied (TRIAGE_CALL_SCRIPT.md) | $0 | 1–3 real engagements identified |

**Success metric:** not money this week — it's ≥1 human reaction per message
sent. Silence data counts: it tells us which message/service to fix.

## Week 2 — first revenue

- Deliver 1–2 **Mess-to-Map ($100 pilot)** or **AI Damage Triage ($250)**
  engagements. Pilot framing: discount for testimonial + referral permission.
- Same-day/48h turnaround is the differentiator. Use the generators; human
  pass on sections 4–5; deliver with appendix + re-verify commands.
- Day 7 checkpoint: if zero engagements after 8+ contacts, STOP and rewrite
  the offer using call/reply data — do not build more tooling.

## Week 3 — repeat what worked

- Follow-up sequence on all non-repliers (FOLLOW_UP_SEQUENCE.md)
- Ask client #1 for testimonial + 1 referral (EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md §4)
- Deliver 1–2 more paid reports; consider the $500 tiers only if someone asks
- Target cumulative: **$450–750**

## Week 4 — consolidation

- Monthly-sweep retainer pitch ($1,500/mo tier) to the most satisfied client
- Publish LAUNCH_POST publicly (REPO_STRATEGY.md phase 2) — inbound starts here
- Target cumulative: **$700–1,500 + 1 retainer conversation**

## Rules that keep this alive

1. **Contact before craft.** If forced to choose between building and sending,
   send. The docs say this; the slapdown says this; winter says this.
2. **Cash services only.** No equity, no revenue share, no "exposure."
3. **Price is fixed and stated first.** No hourly drift, no scope creep.
4. **Every delivery includes the re-verify commands.** The testimonial comes
   from the client proving we didn't hide anything.
5. If a week produces zero outside-human reactions, the bottleneck is the
   message or the venue — change one, not both, and change it the same day.
