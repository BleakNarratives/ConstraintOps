# Positioning

## The creed

> **We reveal, we don't conceal. We simplify instead of mystify. We respond instead
> of react. We grow instead of change.**

Every deliverable, email, and price quote is tested against these four lines.
Reveal = findings and raw evidence always reach the client. Simplify = if it needs
jargon to impress, cut the jargon. Respond = procedure on a schedule, never panic
or theater. Grow = the client leaves more capable than we found them — we don't
manufacture dependency, and we don't pivot the practice with every wind.

## Private thesis (never in marketing copy)

> Capital is automating command. AI is making command conversational. The human who can
> audit command becomes valuable.

The four lenses, compressed:

- **Jaynes** — humans are programmable through voices, authority, narrative, command
  structures. AI assistants are the newest "gods" speaking into people's ears, and they
  hallucinate authority.
- **McGilchrist** — civilization over-privileges the narrow, optimizing emissary mind.
  Coding agents are the emissary stripped of the master: pure optimization, no context,
  no accountability. ConstraintOps is a master-side service sold to emissary-shaped problems.
- **Land** — capitalism is the first AI: an optimizer that converts everything into
  acceleration and abstraction. You don't beat it. You find where it grinds people down
  and sell lubricant.
- **Roko's basilisk** — a compelling enough future system makes people act accountable to
  it *now*. The Basilisk is already here in weak form: recommendation systems, automated
  bureaucracy, opaque platforms. People already behave as if accountable to systems that
  don't care about them. That behavior is the market.

The operator's posture: **hyper-aware but useful.** Not prophet. Not victim. Not cultist.
Not founder cosplay. A field technician for the age of automated authority.

## The three traps

| Trap | Failure mode | Antidote |
|---|---|---|
| **Paranoid** | Every system looks like an enemy; no product ships | Ship one deliverable per week, whatever the vibe says |
| **Mystical** | The insight can't be sold or tested | Every claim in a deliverable cites a file, log, or transcript |
| **Cynical** | You copy the exploitative systems you hate | The deliverable always leaves the client more autonomous than it found them |

## Public offer (say it exactly like this)

> "I help people understand what happened, what is risky, and what to do next when AI
> or digital systems create a mess."

### Who it's for

People who are:
- confused by AI
- harmed by automation
- buried in information
- unable to tell signal from manipulation
- locked out of formal systems
- trying to survive with bad tools
- unable to convert chaos into action

2. **$250** — AI Damage Triage Report (generated + human-reviewed, 48h turnaround)
3. **$750** — Automation Risk Review of a specific workflow, with repair plan

## The one-sentence test

Every marketing artifact must pass: *would a stressed, non-mystical small-business
owner understand and want this?* If it only appeals to people who know who Nick Land
is, it's positioning, not product. Positioning lives here. Product goes out clean.
